About Drip

Drip Vietnamese Cafe is a family-owned café nestled in the heart of Philadelphia's Chinatown, serving authentic Vietnamese flavors with a modern, playful touch.

We started with a simple belief: that a great cup of Vietnamese coffee — brewed slow, served iced, and layered with intention — could become something you look forward to every day. Our café is a place to slow down, connect, and sip something that feels like home.

From our signature Drippy to our rotating seasonal collections, everything is crafted fresh by our team, who pour both skill and heart into every order. We're beyond grateful for every customer who's made us feel like their spot.

Vietnamese Roots

Our coffee culture is deeply rooted in the Vietnamese tradition of slow-drip phin brewing — rich, bold, and ceremonious. We honor that heritage in every cup while making it accessible to all of Philadelphia.

Community First

Voted Philly's Favorite Coffee Shop by the Philadelphia Inquirer in 2025. We couldn't have done it without our loyal regulars, our Chinatown neighbors, and every first-timer who gave us a chance. This one's for you.

From our silky coconut foam to our rotating limited-time flavors, every drink is made fresh to order. No shortcuts — just good ingredients, creative recipes, and a team that genuinely loves what they do.
